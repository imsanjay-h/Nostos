A LITTLE CONTEXT · THE PERSON BEHIND THE COLLECTION

Still becoming.
Never just one thing.

Hey, I’m Sanjay. I’m interested in the connections between stories, ideas, art, music, films, anime, books, and the little things that make a person who they are. I don’t think a person has to fit into one neat definition — and this place doesn’t either.

Why Nostos exists

Nostos is a personal archive of what stays with me: the films I carry around, the worlds I get lost in, the books that rearrange my thoughts, and the interests I haven’t discovered yet. It reflects who I am now, while leaving room for the person I’ll become.

A world in progress

This isn’t meant to be a finished portrait or a catalogue built for everyone else. It’s a place to wander, notice connections, and return to later — a record of changing tastes, growing curiosities, and different versions of the same person.
